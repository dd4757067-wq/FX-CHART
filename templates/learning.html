SELECTED PATTERN

Ascending Triangle

A converging structure with rising lows pressing into a relatively flat resistance area.

How it forms

Price repeatedly tests resistance while buyers create higher lows. A breakout confirms only with context and risk control.

বাংলা

Resistance বারবার test করার সময় higher low তৈরি হলে buyers-এর pressure বোঝা যায়।
